    Pay and unlock

    Payment link is empty. Open config.js, paste a Stripe Payment Link into stripePaymentLink, and deploy again. Until then the button will not charge anyone.

    Refunds for 14 days. Email . Stripe handles the card. We never see the card number.

    What happens when you pay

    1. You leave this site for Stripe’s payment page.
    2. Stripe emails you a receipt.
    3. Stripe sends you back to the members page.
    4. If you want a refund, email us inside the window. We do not argue.
